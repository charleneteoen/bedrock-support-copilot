# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Most products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in original packaging with all accessories. Opened software and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is processed.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 14 days of delivery. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping costs. Photos of the damage speed up the process.
