# Shipping Information

## Shipping options
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. We ship with UPS and FedEx.

## International shipping
We ship to 40 countries. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed as soon as your order ships. Orders in processing status have not left the warehouse yet; processing usually takes 1 business day.
